How to Install Omarchy Apps

  1. Get the Omarchy Store desktop app (pre-installed on Omarchy 4)
  2. Search for any app and click GET
  3. The app installs automatically – no terminal needed

Or browse the full store to see top charts, new releases and featured apps.
