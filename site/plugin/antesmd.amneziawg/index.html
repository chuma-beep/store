Omazia plugin for Omarchy

by antesmd · Verified

Tunnel status, per-tunnel toggles for running several at once, import, rename, QR export, live traffic and drop notifications in the Omarchy bar. Manages AmneziaWG tunnels under /etc/amnezia/amneziawg via awg-quick and a small root helper…
