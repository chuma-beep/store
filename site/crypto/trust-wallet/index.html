Trust Wallet

One wallet for Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and much more.

Trust Wallet supports a huge number of coins and networks, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and BNB Chain. Buy, swap and store your crypto, and connect to apps from your browser. It's the desktop companion to the popular phone app.

Networks

Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, BNB Chain, Base, Polygon

How to install

  1. Open the official Chrome Web Store page.
  2. Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.
  3. Open it from the puzzle-piece icon and create or import a wallet.
