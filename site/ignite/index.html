Ignite for Omarchy

Make an Omarchy installer USB stick in one click with Ignite in the Omarchy Store desktop app: it downloads the newest official Omarchy ISO from iso.omarchy.org, checks its SHA-256, and writes it to your USB drive.

Install Omarchy next to Windows (dual boot)

  1. Turn off BitLocker (Settings, Privacy & security, Device encryption).
  2. Open Disk Management (diskmgmt.msc), right-click C:, Shrink Volume by 51200 MB (50 GB) or 102400 MB (100 GB).
  3. Boot the Omarchy USB, pick your drive and choose Free space install.
  4. In Omarchy, run limine-scan to add Windows to the boot menu.

On Windows or Mac

  1. Download the latest Omarchy ISO.
  2. Install Rufus (Windows) or balenaEtcher (Windows and Mac).
  3. Choose the ISO, choose your USB stick, and press Start or Flash.
