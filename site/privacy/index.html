Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy covers omarchystore.com, the Omarchy Store desktop app, Omarchy Browser and the Omarchy Store browser extension, all made by Omarchy Development Labs.

Omarchy Store browser extension

The extension does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

  • It downloads the public app list from omarchystore.com/catalog.json (or the same file on GitHub if the site is down). That request carries no information about you.
  • It keeps a copy of that list in your browser's local extension storage so it opens fast. Nothing else is stored.
  • When you search with the right-click menu, the text you selected is passed to the side panel inside your browser only. It is never sent anywhere.
  • The Copy button puts an install command on your clipboard. It doesn't read your clipboard.
  • It does not read the pages you visit, track your browsing, use analytics or show ads.

Omarchy Browser

Omarchy Browser keeps your history, cookies and sign-ins on your own computer. It loads the public app list from omarchystore.com for the store panel. Searches you type in the address bar go to DuckDuckGo. We don't collect any data from the browser.

Omarchy Store desktop app and website

The desktop app installs apps with your computer's own package manager and doesn't send us information about what you install. The website may count page visits and downloads in aggregate, may show ads, and stores your look preference (Dark, Glass or White) in your browser.
